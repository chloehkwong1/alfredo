On first launch, Alfredo pins a small **Getting started** panel to
the bottom-right of the window. It's a 5-step checklist that pulses
the right button in the UI when you click each row, so you don't
have to hunt for things on day one.

### What's in it

1. **Add a repo** (⌘⇧R) — pulses the add-repo button.
2. **Create a worktree** (⌘N) — pulses the new-worktree affordance.
3. **(Optional) Configure setup script** — pulses the Scripts tab in
   Repository Settings, with a follow-up pulse on the actual setup
   script field once the dialog opens.
4. **Start an agent** — pulses the agent terminal area.
5. **Open in your IDE** (⌘O) — pulses the open-in-editor button.

If a target isn't visible yet (e.g. "Create a worktree" before
you've added a repo), the row says so and skips the pulse — fix the
prerequisite and try again.

### Dismissing and reopening

Click the **×** in the panel header to hide it. The dismissal sticks
across restarts.

To bring it back: open **Ask Alfredo** (the help search in the
sidebar toolbar) and click **Take the quick-start tour**. The panel
remounts at the bottom-right.

### When to use it

Day-one orientation, or when you forget where something lives.
Outside of those moments most people dismiss it once and never see
it again — which is the intended behaviour. It's not a permanent
HUD, it's a "what next?" affordance.
